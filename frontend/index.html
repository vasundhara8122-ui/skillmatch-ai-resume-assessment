Why SkillMatch AI?

SkillMatch AI analyzes candidate resumes, identifies technical skills, and creates personalized technical assessments. Candidates can demonstrate their knowledge through theory, code analysis, and real programming challenges. Companies can then review detailed skill reports and skill-match information.
